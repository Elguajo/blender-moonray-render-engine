# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be professional, technically honest, and respectful. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.

Examples of expected behavior:

- provide reproducible information when making technical claims;
- assume good intent while still challenging weak evidence;
- respect different experience levels and working environments;
- keep reviews focused on the contribution;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, threats, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors. Serious incidents should be reported privately to the maintainers rather than discussed in a public issue.
